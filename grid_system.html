STAR

A star is a luminous spheroid of plasma held together by its own gravity, glowing brightly because of nuclear fusion in its core. Primarily composed of hydrogen and helium gases, these celestial bodies produce immense amounts of light and heat. While they appear as tiny, twinkling dots in the night sky due to their vast distances from Earth, they vary widely in size, color, and temperature—with blue stars being the hottest and red stars the coolest.
